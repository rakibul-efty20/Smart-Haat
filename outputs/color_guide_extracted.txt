Fresh green theme: where each colour goes, for the storefront and the admin panel
1. The idea
SmartHaat is a supershop-style store, in the spirit of Meena Bazar: clean white surfaces,
product photos and prices first, and one strong brand colour. Green signals freshness and
trust, so it is used for actions and brand moments. Amber is reserved for offers so
discounts catch the eye. A near-black navy anchors the top bar and footer.
Rule of thumb: about 60% white and light neutrals, 30% green, 10% amber and dark
accents.

Page 4 of 5\n---PAGE 5---\n5. Do and don't
Do use green for actions and amber only for offers.
Do keep cards white on the light grey page background.
Do use primary dark for button backgrounds and prices.
Don't use amber for ordinary buttons or headings, and never put white text on amber.
Don't add another brand colour. The status colours above are the only exception.
Don't use pure black. Body text is #1f2937.
6. Where to change the colours in code
All brand colours live in one place: the :root block at the top of src/styles.scss.
Changing a value there updates the navbar, footer, buttons and cards together.
Ready-made classes: btn-primary, btn-outline-primary, btn-accent, bg-soft, bg-
footer, bg-accent, text-accent and footer-link.
